# Draft: panels need an owner

The replay now goes through a durable intervention owner instead of calling assessment helpers with preassembled grades. Role/configuration admission precedes retained outputs; actual byte objectives precede blind panels. Complete per-criterion votes and tie-breaks survive reopening, and cheap failures stay ineligible.

The scoped casting table keeps unknowns visible. Inert role declarations are not authenticated provider resolution. Fixture panel agreement is not accuracy. Without qualified independent observations there is no measured acceptance or escape rate—and no routing default.

The orchestration is connected and testable without spending on evaluated models. Its live qualification remains separate from its ordinary tests and from the original CHANGES-REQUESTED review.
