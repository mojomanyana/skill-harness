# Draft: do not guess the missing fact

Additional structural rules now distinguish a declared overdue checkpoint, same-revision reopened acceptance and an independently evaluated intent violation. Missing or contradictory facts remain coverage issues; a changed requirement is not an escaped defect.

The new records are explicitly versioned rather than slipped into the old case schema. They remain silent hypotheses, not worker control, authenticated labels or deployed alerts. Actual fact/presentation integration is still required.
