# Draft: adoption is not a flag

Adoption predicates now bind exact hypothesis, experiment, candidate, scope, policy and rollback identities. Independent host approval and eligible facts are rechecked at use; the receipt grants no effects and applies only to future orders.

Later observations distinguish changed requirements/artifacts, caught defects and actual post-acceptance escapes. Rollback needs its own approved request and matching current deployment. Nothing automatically activates or rolls back a candidate, and missing lean measurements stay outside claimed complete denominators.
