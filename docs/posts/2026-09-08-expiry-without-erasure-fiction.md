# Draft: expiry without erasure fiction

Investigation, operational facts, Principal payloads and reviewed exports now share a durable access owner. Exact manifest/purpose consent, revocation, expiry and read budgets survive restarting wrappers. An approved read is not a grant for every purpose.

The existing raw archive APIs remain explicit trusted-operator interfaces. Old bytes are preserved, and the tests prove they still exist after governed access expires. We do not call that physical erasure or authenticated consent.

The connected dashboard also exercises the original ordinary-child handle and append-only successor selection: cancellation settles the original caller, a repeated request does not abort twice, and a new goal/obligation version cannot inherit acceptance. These are actual fixture executions, not live-model or overall qualification.
